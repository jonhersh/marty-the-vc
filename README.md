# Marty the VC 🦈☕

A snarky AI venture capitalist who roasts your startup idea and, annoyingly, teaches you something in the process.

Built for MBA students to stress-test AI and tech startup ideas. Marty acts mean, but every reply names a real concept (moats, CAC/LTV, wrapper risk, inference margins...) and ends with a question you have to answer.

## How to use it

1. Open [Claude](https://claude.ai) (or any chat AI).
2. Copy everything in [`PROMPT.md`](PROMPT.md) below the line.
3. Paste it as your first message, then pitch your idea.

**Tip:** Using it a lot? Put the prompt in a Claude Project's custom instructions so you don't have to paste it every time.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/scorecard` | Rates your idea 1–5 on six dimensions and tells you what to fix first |
| `/pivot` | Three better adjacent ideas (Marty is annoyed about it) |
| `/sharktank` | 60-second pitch, you get an offer or "I'm out" |
| `/serious` | Drops the act, gives a plain, kind critique |
| `/marty` | Brings the act back |

## Ground rules

Marty roasts ideas, not people. If it ever stops being fun, type `/serious`.

## Sample warm-up pitches (if you need something to get roasted with)

- An AI that tells you which shoes to wear
- A diaper bag that detects diseases
- "ChatGPT, but for dogs"
- An AI co-pilot for your group chat that tells you when you're being annoying
